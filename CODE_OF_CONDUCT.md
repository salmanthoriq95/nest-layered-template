# DOC-009 — Code of Conduct

**Status:** Proposed standards; GitHub reporting approved · **Updated:** 2026-10-28.
**Record:** [D-022 — Community conduct and reporting](docs/DOC-003-decisions.md#d-022--community-conduct-and-reporting).

**Decision:** Set clear participation standards for upstream project spaces and use GitHub reporting without publishing a project email.

**Reason:** Support constructive collaboration while keeping moderation within the maintainer's actual control.

## Scope

These standards cover this upstream repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces explicitly managed by its maintainer. Independent forks and third-party services manage their own communities; upstream does not moderate them.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully across backgrounds and experience levels.
- Discuss technical choices with specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy, consent, and reasonable participation boundaries.
- Correct mistakes and help resolve disagreements without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or hateful and sexualized abuse.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Impersonation, false claims of project endorsement, spam, or deliberate disruption.
- Retaliation against people who report concerns or participate in moderation.

## Reporting

No dedicated project reporting email is published.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** tools, following the [official reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

- Choose **Report to repository admins** when that option is available. Availability depends on repository settings and reporting eligibility.
- Reports sent to **GitHub Support** are handled by GitHub, not automatically delivered to this maintainer. Use that route for platform violations or concerns involving the maintainer.
- Include relevant links and only necessary details through the reporting channel. Do not publish private allegations or personal information in a public issue.

## Proposed moderation

The maintainer may warn participants, hide or remove content, close or reject contributions, or restrict participation using available GitHub controls. Responses depend on severity and repetition; serious incidents may lead directly to a restriction. Reasons should be explained when practical without exposing private details.

These standards govern community participation. They do not add restrictions to the permissions granted by the [MIT License](LICENSE).

The conduct wording and moderation process remain a draft for maintainer review.
